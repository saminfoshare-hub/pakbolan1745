Who We Are

A trusted bridge to overseas employment

PAK BOLAN INTERNATIONAL connects qualified Pakistani workers — skilled, semi-skilled, and professional — with genuine overseas employment opportunities, guiding every candidate through a transparent, structured recruitment process.

Who We Are

An overseas employment promotion agency based in Pakistan, working directly with international employers to fill genuine manpower requirements.

Our Mission

To connect capable Pakistani workers with fair, verified overseas job opportunities through an honest and transparent process.

Our Vision

To be a leading name in Pakistan's overseas recruitment sector, known for integrity and candidate care.

How It Works

Your recruitment journey

Eight steps from application to deployment.

01
Submit Application

Complete the online form with your details and documents.

02
Profile Screening

Our team reviews your profile against current vacancies.

03
Candidate Shortlisting

Matching profiles are shortlisted for employer review.

04
Interview / Assessment

Shortlisted candidates are contacted for interview.

05
Document Verification

Passport, CNIC, and certificates are verified.

06
Employer Selection

The employer confirms final candidate selection.

07
Visa / Documentation

Visa processing and travel documentation begins.

08
Deployment

Travel arrangements and overseas deployment.
